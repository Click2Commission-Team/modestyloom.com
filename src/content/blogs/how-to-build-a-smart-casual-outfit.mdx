Smart casual frequently causes anxiety because its boundaries appear indefinite. The master key to resolving this tension is the 'High-Low' contrast rule: pair one traditional tailored element with two refined casual staples.

### Recommended Formula:
- **Upper:** Knitted cotton-silk polo under a softly draped unstructured sports coat.
- **Lower:** Pleated wool-flannel trousers cut slightly cropped above the ankle bone.
- **Footwear:** Dark brown suede penny loafers or minimalist leather sneakers with zero overt logos.
