Color psychology plays an indispensable role in how outfits communicate intent. While neutral tones provide the architectural skeleton of a capsule wardrobe, intelligent color accents provide emotional resonance.

### Exploring the Seasonal Chromatic Spectrum

- **Cerulean Cobalt:** A bold, refreshing blue that sharpens navy, black, and beige separates.
- **Pistachio Matcha:** A soothing green hue that delivers freshness to tailored linen and trench coats.
- **Burnt Paprika:** A grounding, rich warm tone that complements gold accessories and camel outers.

Remember: when working with statement colors, limit primary focal points to one or two pieces per ensemble to preserve refined harmony.
