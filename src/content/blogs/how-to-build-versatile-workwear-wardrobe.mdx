Navigating modern workplace dress codes requires versatility. The hybrid work era demands garments that look sharp over high-definition video calls while retaining all-day breathability during commute hours.

### The 7-Piece Workwear Matrix:
1. Two precision-tailored single-breasted blazers (Navy and Charcoal)
2. Three crisp pinpoint Oxford shirting options in optic white and light blue
3. Two pairs of high-rise pleated wool-blend trousers
4. One architectural leather tote capable of carrying a 15-inch laptop
